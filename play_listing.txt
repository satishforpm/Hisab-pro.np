HisabPro – सबै हिसाब, एउटै App

A fast, offline calculator and utility app made for users in Nepal. No login, no account.

• Calculator with brackets, percentage, history, copy & share
• Percentage, Discount, Profit & Loss, VAT (rate editable)
• EMI, Simple Interest, Compound Interest, Salary
• Nepal Land converter: Ropani, Aana, Paisa, Daam, sq ft, sq m, acre, hectare
• Age calculator, Date difference, Add/Subtract days, Weekday, Time difference
• Currency converter (NPR, USD, INR, EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD, AED, SAR, QAR, JPY, CNY) using approximate, editable rates – not live rates
• Unit converter: length, weight, area, temperature, volume, digital
• Nepali & English, Light/Dark mode, search

Works offline. Calculation history stays on your device and can be cleared anytime.
