HisabPro – Privacy Policy

Developer: Satish Sah (Satish Dev). Website: https://satishdev15.netlify.app. Phone: +9779705970615.

Data we collect: none. HisabPro needs no account, and the app does not request internet access in this version. All calculations happen on your device.

Data stored on your device: calculator history, language, theme and exchange-rate edits are stored locally on your device only. You can delete history anytime with "Clear History" in the app, or by clearing app data/uninstalling.

Sharing: we do not send your data to any server. If you use Share, your chosen text is passed to the app you select. Links to social media open in your browser, which follows that site's own policy.

Currency rates: built-in rates are approximate and not live.

Changes: if a future version adds online features (e.g. live currency rates), this policy will be updated first.

Contact: use the website above.
